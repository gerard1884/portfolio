Background

I came to DevOps from the network up.

Most infrastructure problems that look like application problems turn out to be routing, DNS, certificates, or policy. Starting in Cisco networking before moving into cloud gave me a habit of checking the layer everyone else skips.

Day to day that means Terraform for anything that should be reproducible, Kubernetes for anything that should heal itself, and Prometheus and Grafana so a regression shows up on a dashboard instead of in someone’s inbox.

I’m based in Canada and I still lab things out in GNS3 before they touch production.
